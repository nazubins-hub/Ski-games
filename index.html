Ski games

Small games and tools for teaching skiing. They run in the browser on a phone or a laptop, with nothing to install.

  • Edge rate

    Tip your skis on edge with a slider and link turns through the gates. Change edge angle progressively. Move it abruptly and you fall.

    Rate: the speed at which movement occurs. ATM, Chapter 6

  • Rotary

    Turn your skis with a slider. Down the hill you speed up, across the hill you slow down. Hold a steady speed as the pitch changes.

    Turn shape controls speed.
