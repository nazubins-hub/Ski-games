Magnitude of Pressure

Control how much pressure the skis build against the snow. Two thumbs: Edge tips the skis, Rotate turns them. Use them alone and together and watch the skis bend, the tracks they leave and the push from the snow.

PSIA-AASI Alpine fundamental: “Regulate the magnitude of pressure created through ski/snow interaction.”

  • Edge only: with enough edge, a carve. The sidecut bends the ski into an arc; more edge, tighter turn, more pressure.
  • Rotate only: a pivot slip. The flat skis slide sideways and add almost no pressure.
  • Both, the same way: a skid. The edge bites into the slide, pressure builds and speed scrubs off. Rotate a lot, then edge quickly: an edge set.
  • Rotate against the edge: once the edges engage, it can’t turn the skis that way. It steadies them along the edge instead, so any skid dies out and the skis carve.
  • Speed multiplies it all. Drag the view to look from any side.
